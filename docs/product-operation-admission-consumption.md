# Consumer adoption gate

A product consumer may adopt `product-operation-admission-binding.mjs` only when it loads the AccessGrant and billing admission from its installed authority stores. Request bodies may identify the requested operation, but they may not supply a currentness object, an admitted boolean, an admin bypass, a rate card, a budget decision or an effect authorization.

Consumer acceptance requires exact source generation, accepted `main`, installed bytes, running generation, authorized provider attempt, independent readback, consumed return and restart/reopen evidence.
