The SDK owns public atomic behavior and accessibility primitives. Publisher composes larger reusable surfaces. Articles supplies editorial meaning, source roles and comprehension requirements. Product adopters keep their own domain semantics and visual hierarchy.

The composition boundary

Meaning → composition → behavior → adopter.

  1. ArticlesStory, audience, voice, source roles, evidence and comprehension.
  2. PublisherTarget-neutral shell, panel, publication and placement composition.
  3. SDKAtomic interaction, state, accessibility and callable public primitives.
  4. ProductDomain semantics, product-local hierarchy, target adapter and real runtime proof.

Boundary: examples prove the intended composition grammar. They do not claim that every product adopter, provider effect or deployment is running or live.

TEMPLATEPRODUCT STORY

Product story

A useful product page should answer the reader’s questions in order instead of dumping framework nouns into cards.

  1. WHYWhat human problem exists?
  2. WHATWhat does this product own?
  3. HOWHow does it reduce work?
  4. CURRENTWhat is actually true today?
  5. ACTIONWhat can I safely do now?
  6. PROOFHow do I know the claim is real?
